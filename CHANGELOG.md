All notable changes to SafeOS Guardian will be documented in this file.

The format is based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.0.0/),
and this project adheres to [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html).

## [Unreleased]

### Added
- Settings opens on one list of its pages: Detection, Alert sounds and Quiet hours first, then Alerts, Camera picture and This device, and Your own server while a server address is saved. Each row says its value ("Standard, motion 80, sound 80", "Volume 100", "Off"), and the first three carry a small picture of it. The section chips, the panels and Save Changes leave the page, and each control they held has a page of its own. The alert volume stands at the top of Alert sounds with a key that plays the Alert tone. A new page, Data on this device, counts the alerts, saved pictures, pets and sounds this device keeps, exports the settings as `safeos-settings-<yyyy-mm-dd>.json`, and asks in place before it clears all data, with no browser confirm box. A new page, Your own server, holds the server's address and the name sent to it, with its own Save; a session made on this device alone sends nothing and the page says so, and a clear of all data while the name saves leaves no copy of it here. A browser that refuses storage, or a device too full to take the settings, keeps each change for the visit and says it was not saved, at the top of every settings page, and the settings store does not throw. The export holds the look and text and the notification choices too, and the count takes in Wildlife sightings. Turning off Send browser notifications on Notifications stops browser notifications for alerts, as the list's Notifications row says. Preferences an earlier version saved on this browser are read once, so a profile read later does not undo a volume set since.
- The dashboard says whether this device is watching: "Not watching", or "Watching in another tab" while another tab of the same browser watches. One key, **Start watching**, asks for the camera while the dashboard is on screen and starts the watch on the way's own page with no second press. The line above it names the way it starts and when the last watch ended, or the last moment it was known to run.
- The dashboard lists the four ways to watch in one order, Motion and sound, Lost and Found, People and Wildlife, each with one line; Lost and Found shows the saved pet's photo. Before setup the same dashboard shows **Start setup**, and each way's row opens setup with that way named.
- **Since you last looked** lists the alerts of all four ways that are not marked as seen, newest first, with their pictures, and **Mark all as seen** marks them. A Lost and Found sighting the watch raised is listed as Lost and Found's ("Colours like {name}’s") with its kept close-up, and is marked in the watch's list; a device whose newest alert picture found no room says it is out of room. On a device that has not answered the notice about alert pictures, the dashboard's start key opens it before the watch, and its **Go back** closes the camera the dashboard opened. An alert card or History row whose saved time does not parse shows the time its id carries, never the time it was read. A Motion and sound alert whose event repeated is listed at its newest alert, as History lists it. A device out of room that refuses **Mark all as seen** says it is out of room. History's link in the header and in the phone bar carries their count. The frames of one stay in view are one alert, and alerts saved before this version do not count as new.
- The app's pages share one header (Dashboard, History, Settings, Help and the look switch, with More when the row runs short), a bar of Dashboard, History and Settings at the foot of a phone screen, and the site's footer. On a phone the look switch and Help are the first two rows of Settings. The header, the bar and the footer are in the served HTML and show before any script runs.
- History lists Wildlife's sightings, and marks every new alert as seen when it opens.
- Open tabs tell each other when an alert is saved, marked as seen or deleted, and when a watch starts or ends, so one watch runs on a device at a time: a second start asks "Watch here instead? The other tab stops." A tab holds its start from the press until the way's watch begins, so of two tabs whose start keys are pressed at once one starts and the other asks. A browser with no BroadcastChannel passes the words through local storage (`safeos-tab-message`), and the watching tab keeps a note of its watch there (`safeos-tab-watch`). A sighting deleted or History cleared in one tab stays so when a watching Wildlife tab saves its next sighting, and the frames the limit removes leave every tab's list.
- Each new Lost and Found frame records whether it raised the alert (`alerted`).
- Three local storage keys, `safeos-last-way`, `safeos-history-seen` and `safeos-watch-session`, which Clear All Local Data removes.
- `/help/#camera` and `/help/#notifications` open Troubleshooting at those entries.
- `apps/guardian-ui/scripts/app-checks.mjs` walks the app's pages in Chromium on the built static site for the pull request's own build.
- The watch opens with **Watch for**: **Motion and sound** or **Lost and Found**, above its camera and its settings. Motion and sound sounds an alert when something moves or makes a sound; Lost and Found also compares what the camera sees with a saved pet's photos, by colour and shape, while the watch runs. With Lost and Found chosen the watch shows that pet: its first saved photo, its name, its count of photos, **Change pet**, which opens **Choose a pet**, and the line on what it compares and its limits; with no pet saved, "No pet saved" and **Add a pet**, which opens the Lost & Found page. Choosing Motion and sound during a watch stops the comparison and Lost and Found starts it, with no restart of the camera, and a polite status says so. The choice is setup's own (`lostFoundChosen` in the onboarding store), so setup and the watch show the same one whatever the use, and the Lost & Found page's **Start Monitoring** opens the watch with Lost and Found and its pet. The pet is kept as `chosenPetId` in the Lost & Found store, so a reload keeps the choice and the pet; until one is chosen it is the newest saved pet, a thing saved as an object is the watch's only once chosen, and the Lost & Found page offers no **Start Monitoring** for a person an earlier version saved. In **Choose a pet** the arrow keys move the check and a press or Enter chooses; Escape and **Close** change nothing. The page's title reads "Motion and sound | SafeOS" or "Lost and Found | SafeOS". From 1024 px the pet's card heads the right column, and from 1440 px its other photos stand under it.

The Pictures workflow captures the watch with Lost and Found chosen, with no pet saved and with a pet, at 390 and 1280 px in both looks.

The question says to run setup again from the home page to change it, in place of naming Settings, which has no such control. The choices carry `aria-pressed`. Finishing setup opens the last key's page alone: the forwarding for a person who arrives with setup done stands down as the last step finishes.

A second missing file within five minutes shows that message instead of reloading again.

A sighting reads "Could this be {name}?". While Lost and Found compares, its meter shows the pet and says it compares with the pet’s photos. It shows no bar or number. The dashboard's **Start watching** opens the camera alone when the camera is allowed and the microphone is blocked.
